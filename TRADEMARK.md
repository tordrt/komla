# Komla Trademark Policy

Komla's source code is free software under the [AGPL](LICENSE). The name "Komla", the Komla logo and the komla.dev domain are not. They belong to Tafjord Invest AS, and they tell people that what they're getting is Komla as we make it. This policy says how you can use them.

## Without asking

- Refer to Komla by name, such as "works with Komla", "a plugin for Komla" or "a fork of Komla".
- Say a project is based on Komla, as long as it has its own name.
- Write articles, reviews, videos and tutorials about Komla, and use the logo to illustrate them.
- Redistribute official Komla builds unmodified, under the Komla name.
- Package Komla for an operating system or package manager under the Komla name, if you build it from unmodified source and change only what packaging needs. Make clear that the package doesn't come from us.

## Not without our written permission

- Distribute a modified version of Komla under the Komla name or with the Komla logo.
- Use "Komla", or a name that's easy to confuse with it, in the name of your product, company, domain or account.
- Use the Komla logo, or something that looks like it, as an app icon or product logo.
- Suggest that we make, endorse or support something we don't.
- Sell merchandise with the Komla name or logo.

## If you fork Komla

Give your fork its own name, icon and bundle ID, and replace the Komla name and logo everywhere they appear in the app, including the window title, the sidebar and the macOS permission prompts. You may describe it as "based on Komla". Don't present it as an official build, and don't make it identify itself to Komla's online services as one.

## Questions

If you're unsure whether something is allowed, or want permission for something this policy doesn't allow, write to us through [komla.dev/contact](https://komla.dev/contact).
